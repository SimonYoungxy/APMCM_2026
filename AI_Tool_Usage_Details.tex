\section*{2. 使用目的与环节 / Purpose and stages of use}

AI was used only as an auxiliary aid, in three narrow roles: (i) implementing the Python
analysis code from the team's written specification; (ii) generating figures and \LaTeX{}
tables from the exported results; (iii) drafting English prose from the team's outline.

The modelling framework, the choice of every statistical method, the definition of each
estimand and the interpretation of all results were determined and carried out by the team.
AI did not generate or invent data, references, numerical results or conclusions.

\section*{3. 关键交互记录 / Key interactions}

\noindent\textbf{(a) Paired structure of the playback trials.}
\emph{Prompt:} ``Check whether the playback trials are independent, and if not, whether the
analysis accounts for it.'' \emph{Reply:} the assistant reported that the 278 trials form
139 natural-call/control pairs matched on date, context, distance and group size, and
computed McNemar $\chi^2=66.33$, $p=3.8\times10^{-16}$, paired odds ratio $37.0$.
\emph{Team action:} the paired test was adopted for the overall response comparison and the
independence assumption was rewritten to disclose that the regression models remain
unclustered.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{(b) Multiple testing across measures.}
\emph{Prompt:} ``Report both the nominal and the corrected significance of the six acoustic
measures.'' \emph{Reply:} a Benjamini--Hochberg correction across the six omnibus tests was
implemented. \emph{Team action:} the correction was adopted and the abstract and conclusions
were aligned so that only the features surviving correction are called significant.

\section*{4. 采纳内容与人工修改 / Adopted content and human modification}

The analysis code, figures and tables produced with AI assistance were adopted after the
team had independently checked every exported number against the raw data. The English draft
was revised sentence by sentence against the results. Corrections made by the team during
review include: replacing an independent two-sample comparison with the paired McNemar test;
adding the Benjamini--Hochberg correction; correcting the stated design size of the future
playback experiment to four call-type levels $\times$ thirteen distances; enforcing an
events-per-variable budget before fitting; and replacing hard-coded paths so that the
pipeline reproduces from the supplied data.
